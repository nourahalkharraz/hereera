# برومبتات خلفيات الغلاف — دعوة سند ونورة

ستّةٌ، كلٌّ منها ملتزمٌ بقواعد التوليد الخمس في `PROMPT-TEMPLATE.md`:
سطر `REALISM` صريح، ولونُ أرضٍ لا أبيضُ قصٍّ (فالخلفيّة لا تُقصّ)،
وفراغٌ محجوزٌ في الوسط لموضع الشعار وتحته «اسحب لفتح الدعوة».

الألوان مأخوذةٌ من لوحة الدعوة لا مختارةً بالعين:
الورق `#FAF6ED` `#EDE8DC` `#E4DDCE` — الزيتون `#818263` `#4D5033`
— الزهريّ `#EFD7CF` `#DDBAAE`.

الدعوةُ كلُّها واقفةٌ عند واقعيّة ٤: خطٌّ مسطّحٌ بلا ظلّ. وما جاوز ٦
يجعل الشعارَ يبدو مقصوصًا ملصوقًا عليه.

## ١ · نقشٌ محفور — واقعية ٤

```
Create ONE vertical 1080x1920 background image, fully bleeding to all
edges, ground color #FAF6ED filling the entire canvas.

Composition: delicate botanical engraving confined to the outer edges
only — a soft cluster at the top-left corner, a smaller answering
cluster at the bottom-right, thin trailing stems along the side
margins. The entire central area must remain empty, plain #FAF6ED.

REALISM: 4 out of 10. Botanically accurate proportions — a real olive
leaf, a real five-petal blossom, correct venation — but rendered as
pure outline with NO shading, NO volume, NO cast shadow, NO
highlight, NO depth. Flat ink on flat paper, the way a scientific
herbarium plate is drawn. It should look observed from life yet
printed, never photographed and never invented.

STYLE: fine-line botanical engraving, uniform hairline strokes of
constant weight, olive ink on warm cream, calm and restrained.
Colors: #FAF6ED #818263 #4D5033 ONLY.

NO photorealism, NO 3D render, NO puffy stickers, NO cartoon, NO
thick white outlines, NO bright candy colors, NO gradients inside the
shapes, NO frame or border, NO vignette.
No text, no letters, no numbers.
```

```

Composition: the flat surface of handmade cotton rag paper — faint

cream, matte, no gloss, no lighting direction.

Extremely subtle — small difference between lightest and darkest
area. NO depicted objects, NO 3D render, NO stickers, NO cartoon, NO
```

```
STYLE: fine-line botanical engraving, uniform hairline strokes, olive

Colors: #FAF6ED #818263 #EFD7CF ONLY.

Very low contrast and very sparse — when tiled it must read as faint

puffy stickers, NO cartoon, NO thick white outlines, NO bright candy
```
