## Full description

**You did not start a business to write invoices from scratch at 11pm.**

But here you are — rewriting the same proposal for the fourth time, guessing at a price, and hoping this client is not the one who disappears after delivery.

The problem is not you. It is that nobody hands you the paperwork side of running a business. So you improvise it, every single time, and it costs you hours you will never bill for.

This toolkit is the paperwork, already written.

### What you get

**1. Pricing Calculator** *(spreadsheet)*
Enter your real costs and hours. It tells you your minimum hourly rate, day rate, and what to quote for a specific project — including the buffer and the profit margin most people forget. If your current rate is below the number it gives you, you now know exactly why the money never adds up.

**2. Client Intake Form** *(PDF)*
Send it before the first call. It asks about budget, timeline, scope and who signs off — so you stop giving free consultations to people who were never going to hire you.

**3. Client Proposal** *(PDF)*
Three-tier structure so the question becomes *which one* instead of *whether*. Includes scope inclusions **and exclusions** — the section that saves you from unpaid extra work.

**4. Service Agreement** *(PDF)*
Seventeen plain-language clauses covering scope, deposits, late payment, revision limits, ownership, cancellation and the client who goes quiet for a month. Written to be read, not to intimidate.

**5. Invoice Template** *(PDF)*
Clean, itemised, with payment terms and late-fee wording already in place. The kind of invoice that gets paid without a follow-up email.

**6. Client Onboarding Checklist** *(PDF)*
Every step from first enquiry to testimonial request. Run it for every client and a one-person business starts to feel like an established one.

**7. Income & Expense Tracker** *(spreadsheet)*
Log payments and costs; the summary sheet builds itself. Month-by-month profit, profit margin, best month, and expenses already sorted into categories for tax time.

**8. Monthly Content Planner** *(PDF)*
Four content pillars, a month of posts planned in one sitting, and a section for what actually worked so you stop guessing.

**Plus: Start Here guide** — the order to use everything in, and why that order matters.

### Formats

- Every PDF in **A4 and US Letter**
- Spreadsheets in **.xlsx** — works in Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers
- Print them, or fill them on screen
- **Instant download.** Yours forever, including future updates.

### Who this is for

Freelancers, designers, photographers, VAs, coaches, consultants, cleaners, bakers, tutors, shop owners — anyone selling their own work who is tired of improvising the business side.

### Who this is not for

Large teams needing accounting software. This is for one to three people who need real documents, today, without a subscription.

## FAQ section

**Can I edit these?**
Yes. The spreadsheets are fully editable. The PDFs are designed to be filled in on screen or printed and written on.

**What software do I need?**
Any PDF reader, and Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers for the two spreadsheets. Nothing to install, no subscription.

**Is this a one-time payment?**
Yes. One payment, yours forever, including any updates I make.

**Can I use these with my clients?**
Yes — that is the point. Use them in your own business as much as you like. You cannot resell or redistribute the files themselves.

**Will this work outside the US?**
The documents are country-neutral and every PDF comes in A4 and US Letter. Tax rates and legal requirements vary by country, so check the contract and the tax field against your local rules.
